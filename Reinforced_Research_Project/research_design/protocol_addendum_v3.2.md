# Protocol addendum v3.2 (Stage 7) — written and committed BEFORE any surrogate was fitted to the new problems
Written 2026-11-05, after the Stage 6 results, the three simulated review rounds and the final audit. Motivation: the weaknesses of the Stage 6 paper that new data can address are (i) only 8 third-party problems from 4 families, (ii) the solvers take milliseconds per run, so the cost–benefit claim was never measured on a case where simulation is expensive, (iii) the default pool contains no neural network and the tool never recommends one, (iv) the tuned network was given a small search (16 trials, a narrowed space) and may be under-trained. Weaknesses that new data cannot address (a classical pool, a known qualitative finding, no named authors) stay and are stated in the paper.

**What was looked at before this file was written.** Run times and value ranges of the new solvers (12 random solves each, to check they run and are finite) and 10 unit tests of the new solvers (mesh convergence, exact scaling/symmetry identities, Newton consistency). The unit tests exposed one bug in `disc_elast` (the clamped and loaded regions were selected by facet midpoints without restricting to boundary facets, so interior wedges were clamped/loaded and the solution did not converge under mesh refinement). It was fixed before any surrogate was fitted (deviation V14). No error of any surrogate on any new problem had been seen. The families were chosen for physical diversity (wave equation near resonance, nonlinear reaction–diffusion solved by Newton, elasticity on a curved domain, advection in a cellular flow) and two of them deliberately contain hard regimes (Helmholtz level 2 near several resonances; reaction–diffusion with strong reaction). They were not chosen by trying surrogates on them.

**Frozen.** The tool (`surroselect/`), the benchmark package, all criteria below and the data protocol are frozen at the commit that contains this file (its hash is recorded in `research_design/freeze_v3.2.txt` before the first run). Changes to the tool since the Stage 6 results, all made before the freeze: an optional `--with-network` flag in the CLI (adds `TunedMLP` to the pool; default pool unchanged), optional `depth_range` and `widths` arguments of `TunedMLP` (defaults equal the Stage 6 search space, so Stage 6 behaviour is unchanged), `get_problem` lookup in the benchmark package.

## 7.1 Problems (scikit-fem 12.0.2, `problems_tp2.py`)
Eight new instances, four families × two levels (data protocol as before: pool 4096, test 1000, extrapolation 500, nested training subsets `default_rng(1000+seed).permutation(4096)[:N]`):
`helmholtz_L1/L2` (p = 3 / 4, m = 256; wave number² in [2,20] / [30,70], source position, level 2 also source width), `reacdiff_fe_L1/L2` (p = 4 / 6, m = 256; nonlinear reaction–diffusion −∇·(D∇u) + r u³ = s by Newton; level 2 has 2×2 diffusion blocks and strong reaction), `disc_elast_L1/L2` (p = 5 / 9, m = 82; plane stress on a disc, 4 / 8 angular stiffness sectors, load angle), `advdiff_cell_L1/L2` (p = 3 / 4, m = 256; advection–diffusion in a divergence-free cellular flow, level 2 thinner layers and source width). One further problem, `lshape_fine_L1` (p = 4, m = 130), is the L-plate of Stage 6 on a mesh refined 7 times (about 1.5 s per run on this machine) and is used only in experiment E7.

## 7.2 Experiments
* **T7**: the five legacy methods (B = 8, 1500 epochs) on the 8 new problems, N ∈ {64, 256, 1024}, seeds 0–2.
* **C7**: `SurrogateSelector` with the default pool exactly as in v3.0 (4-fold CV, tie band 0.1 decade, `refit_all=True`), same N and seeds.
* **S7**: `TunedMLP` as in v3.0 (16 trials, depth 2–4, width 64–256), N ∈ {256, 1024}, seeds 0–2.
* **S+ (stronger network, new)**: `TunedMLP(n_trials=48, depth_range=(2,5), widths=(64,128,256,512))`, N = 256, seeds 0–2, on all **16** third-party problems (the 8 of Stage 6 and the 8 new). Purpose: test whether the Stage 6 network results were limited by the small search. The search epochs, final retraining (4× epochs) and L-BFGS polish are as in S.
* **N7 (tool + network)**: `SurrogateSelector(candidates=default pool + [TunedMLP(16 trials)])` with `refit_all=True`, N = 256, seeds 0–2, on the 8 new problems. The network is one more pool member chosen by the same 4-fold CV.
* **E7 (expensive solver)**: on `lshape_fine_L1`, 512 training-pool runs and 300 independent test runs are computed (the parameter draws follow the same hash-seeded protocol). N ∈ {64, 256}, seeds 0–2 (training subsets from the pool of 512). The tool (default pool, `refit_all=True`), always-POD+GP (matern52, r = 32) and the tuned network (16 trials, N = 256) are fitted. Measured: seconds per solver run (median of 20), seconds of the tool (fit + learning curve), the break-even number of surrogate queries (N·t_sim + t_tool)/t_sim, forecast quality from N0 = 64 to 256.

## 7.3 Criteria (cells = problem × N, seed-mean test errors; thresholds are those of v3.0 unchanged)
Applied to the 8 new problems alone (24 cells for S1–S3, 16 for S4):
* **S1** median regret ≤ 1.5 and ≥ 75 % of cells ≤ 2.0 (regret = err(selected)/min over pool of test error). **S2** median err(selected)/err(legacy POD+GP of T7) ≤ 1.0. **S3** median err(selected)/err(L) ≤ 1.0 (L = the legacy method with the smallest validation error per problem × N × seed) and more cells better by > 10 % than worse by > 10 %. **S4** the strong network (S7) better than the tool-selected model by more than 25 % in at most 4 of 16 cells.
* **B1** forecast of the selected model's test error at 4·N0 within a factor 2 of the same candidate refit on 4·N0 runs, in ≥ 70 % of the 48 cases (N0 ∈ {64, 256}). **B2** reach-target classification (1 % and 0.1 %) from N0 = 256, accuracy ≥ 75 % over 24 cases; reported together with the majority-class baseline. **F1** where `basis_limited` is True, the best full-field GP beats the best POD-based member by > 10 % in ≥ 75 % of those cells (evaluable if ≥ 5 flagged cells).
* **PT** the reduced-basis family clearly better (ȳ > 0.10, validation-selected) at N = 1024 on at least 5 of the 8 new problems.
* **W1 (tool + network, new)**: over the 8 cells (problem at N = 256): median regret against the best member of the enlarged pool (including the network) ≤ 1.5 and ≥ 75 % of cells ≤ 2.0. **W2**: err(tool + network) ≤ 1.1 × min(err(tool alone, C7), err(network alone, S7)) in at least 6 of 8 cells.
* **E1 (expensive case)**: the tool's regret ≤ 2.0 at both N (seed means); forecast from N0 = 64 to 256 within a factor 2 in at least 2 of 3 seeds. The cost numbers are reported without a criterion.
* **S5 (descriptive, no threshold)**: number of the 16 problems at N = 256 in which the stronger network S+ beats the tool-selected model by more than 25 %, versus the same count for the standard network S (Stage 6 and S7 data).
* **Pooled (descriptive)**: S1–S4, B1–B2, F1 recomputed over the 16 third-party problems (Stage 6 and Stage 7), with problem-level cluster-bootstrap intervals; the 8 new problems alone are the held-out confirmation of the frozen tool, the Stage 6 problems were seen when the tool was tuned (see v3.0 §6.4 and V9).

## 7.4 Predictions stated now (so that they can be wrong)
S1 will probably hold; S2 will probably hold; S3 is marginal (the legacy comparison was only just met in Stage 6) and may fail; B1 will probably hold from N0 = 64 and may fail from N0 = 256 (it did in 9 of 24 cases in Stage 6); B2 will probably not beat the majority-class baseline by much; PT will probably **not** hold (the Stage 6 result was 3 of 8); the basis-limit flag will probably fire on `helmholtz_L2` and possibly on `advdiff_cell_L2`; `helmholtz_L2` is the likeliest source of a large regret; the stronger network will probably win more cells than the standard one (S5), which would mean that the Stage 6 network result was partly limited by tuning; W1 will probably hold and W2 is uncertain; E1: the regret will probably be small because only two contested models matter at the ends of the budget range, the forecast is uncertain.

## 7.5 Rules
A failed fit counts as infinite error; no criterion, pool member, grid or data protocol is changed after the first result of the experiment concerned; deviations are logged in `research_design/deviations_v2.md` (V14 onwards). Criteria are not tuned to results. Any analysis added after seeing results is labelled post hoc. Results on 8 problems from 4 further families are descriptive of those families, not general. Environment: the same machine class as before (4 cores, `OMP_NUM_THREADS=1`).
