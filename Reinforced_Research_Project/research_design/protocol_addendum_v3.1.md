# Protocol addendum v3.1 — real-data check (pre-registered BEFORE any model is fitted to the real datasets)
Motivation: all earlier results are on simulated problems. The working instructions ask for at least one condition on real data, reported honestly even if the result weakens. Two real, measured, openly licensed datasets are used; both have **scalar outputs and measurement noise**, so they violate assumptions A2 (deterministic outputs) and are not field data. The check therefore asks only whether the tool still behaves sensibly when its assumptions are strained. Nothing about the datasets' behaviour under any model had been seen when this was written; only shapes, ranges and licences were inspected.
## Data (`data_real/`, CC BY 4.0, UCI repository)
* **airfoil** (NASA wind-tunnel, 1,503 rows, 5 inputs → sound pressure level). Inputs used as given except log10 of frequency and of suction-side displacement thickness (both span about two decades; decided now).
* **ccpp** (combined-cycle power plant, 9,568 rows, 4 inputs → net electrical output).
## Protocol
Fixed random split (seed 12345): 30 % test, 70 % pool. Training subsets by seed permutation of the pool, N ∈ {64, 256, 1024} (airfoil: pool 1,052 so N = 1024 is nearly the whole pool; reported with that caveat), seeds 0–2. Error metric: **normalised RMSE** (new `metric="nrmse"` option of the tool: ‖Y − Ŷ‖_F / ‖Y − mean(Y_train)‖_F), because per-sample relative error is meaningless for outputs with large offsets; the default metric of the tool is unchanged. Methods: the tool (default pool, `metric="nrmse"`), always-POD+GP (matern52, r = 32), the strongly tuned network (`TunedMLP`, 16 trials, same settings, `metric="nrmse"`), oracle = best pool member by test error.
## Criteria (descriptive; no threshold is a claim of success)
R1: median over cells (2 datasets × 3 N = 6) of err(selected)/err(oracle) ≤ 1.5 (same as S1). R2: err(selected) ≤ err(always-GP) in at least 4 of 6 cells. R3: strongly tuned network better than the tool-selected model by > 25 % in at most 2 of 6 cells. R4 (forecast): 4N0 forecast within a factor 2 for N0 = 64 and 256 in ≥ 70 % of the 12 cases (2 datasets × 2 N0 × 3 seeds; for airfoil, N0 = 256 → 1024 uses the same nearly-full pool).
**Prediction stated now:** R1 and R3 will probably hold; R4 will probably fail because measurement noise creates an error floor that a power law cannot extrapolate. Whatever happens is reported.
