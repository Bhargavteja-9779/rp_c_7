# Project details (section 0 of the working instructions) — filled from the project record; unknown items are marked and must be supplied by the authors
* **Topic and problem.** Choosing a fast stand-in (surrogate) for a slow parametric simulation when only N runs (32–1,024) can be afforded: Gaussian-process / reduced-basis / kernel / polynomial models versus tuned neural networks; how many runs are needed for a target accuracy; when the POD basis limits accuracy. Contribution: a controlled 44-problem study (36 problems from our own solvers, 8 from the independent finite-element library scikit-fem) and a free selection tool, SurroSelect, with a pre-registered evaluation.
* **Application the paper is framed around.** Simulation-based design and uncertainty analysis in engineering (flow through porous rock, heat conduction, pollutant transport, elastic plates, thermal runaway, waves). All data are simulated; no field data were used.
