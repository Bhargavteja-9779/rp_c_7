# Novelty reassessment (Phase 4 summary)
See `literature/competing_work_analysis.md` and `literature/updated_literature_matrix.csv`.
* **v1 novelty claim:** "no comparison of POD+GP/POD+poly vs neural under identical tuning; no a-priori pilot rule; no tuning-asymmetry quantification."
* **Status after re-investigation:** the first item is **weakened** (kernel/GP competitiveness was already published — Batlle et al. 2024 — and was missing from v1); the second and third are **unrefuted but search-limited**.
* **Defensible contribution (conditional on v2 results):** (i) a pre-registered matched-search, tie-aware comparison with a mechanism test of why POD+GP wins; (ii) an *out-of-sample* test of a cheap pilot diagnostic; (iii) a measured tuning-asymmetry bias. If Exp A does not replicate, (ii) is reported as a negative result.
* **Claims that must not appear:** "first", "new principle", general guidance for practitioners, state-of-the-art accuracy.
