# Independent design and statistics audit (agent 3/4)

Scope: v1 study (16 instances), Exp A (8 instances), Exp D, the pre-registration documents, the audit files and the static manuscript sections (`manuscript/static_v3.md`). Experiments B, A2 and E were not analysed: no raw_expB data, A2 outputs or E outputs were read. A2/E are criticised only as pre-registered designs. No existing file was modified and no experiment was run. Every number below was recomputed from the raw JSON/CSV files with single-threaded numpy/pandas/scipy, unless marked "from the docs". I also read `code/src/surrogate_bench/{hpo,methods,experiment,analysis,stats,data,diagnostics}.py` and the v2 `PodKRR`.

Helper scripts are in the session scratchpad `/tmp/claude-0/-home-user-rp-c-7/d247ddca-2eb6-5163-bca0-57b556b9e2f0/scratchpad/` (`load.py`, `a1.py` to `a3.py`). They are not part of the project.

Verdict in one line: the numbers reported in the JSON files are arithmetically correct, but several design features not discussed in the docs weaken the *interpretation* ("linear subspace vs neural", "tuning matched", "diagnostic not replicated", "H4a confirmed"). I found no FATAL issue (no leakage, no fabrication). I found three CRITICAL design issues (F01 to F03).

## 2. Claims that I consider adequately supported

1. **Stage-1 confirmatory outcomes are correctly reported as negative or mixed.** H1a (median ȳ(N=32) = -0.059, one-sided Wilcoxon p = 0.874), H1b (k = 5/16, p = 0.96), H2 (Holm 0.1446, so not confirmed) and the Holm arithmetic (4 tests) all reproduce. H4a's arithmetic (b = 10, c = 1, p = 0.00586, Holm 0.0234) is right (its *interpretation* is F09).
2. **Tie inflation.** 28/96 v1 cells are ties (29%). At N=32 there are 12 cells with ȳ < 0 but only 5 NL-clear. The statement "7 of the 12 N=32 'wins' are ties" is correct.
3. **Exp A primary numbers.** P1 ρ = -0.381, exact p = 0.1799; P2 gain 0.598, RMSE 0.976 → 0.595; S1 6/10 correct; seed sign consistency 50%; tie counts by N. All reproduce. "Not replicated under the pre-registered decision rule" is a correct statement of the rule's outcome.
4. **Exp D's negative result.** "D1 rejected" is robust at cell and at instance level (median 14.3; 13.5% ≤ 3; instance-level 21% ≤ 3). The statement "a random-search isotropic KRR at B = 8 does not reproduce POD+GP" is supported.
5. **Validation-based family winner is not the source of the effect** (F12). Trial-level and family-level regret versus oracle are ≤ 0.02 decades for N ≥ 64 and the error is not asymmetric.
6. **No train/test leakage in selection.** `hpo.py` picks the trial with min validation error; test error is only stored. Metric aggregation (median/p95) does not change classes (0/89 sign flips).
7. **Qualitative regime picture within the protocol.** Among the 24 instances, POD+GP-type surrogates are better by ≥ 1 decade on the smooth p = 3 problems at N ≥ 256, and NL wins narrowly (≤ 0.44 decades) on a few hard (L2, transport/front/soliton) instances. Reservation: this holds *within this NN protocol* (F02, F04).
8. **Tuning-asymmetry bias exists at the cell level and is tie-robust**: clear-NL-win cells flip 13 vs 0. The effect is concentrated in 6 instances.
9. **Disclosure quality.** Deviations (D1-D5), the seen-before-registration lists, the addendum's honesty note and "post hoc in motivation" are candid. The hash-seeding bug is disclosed and the replay check (20/20) is credible. Residual criticism is F14.

## 3. Claims that I consider overstated or unsupported

1. **"Linear-subspace vs neural" as the explanatory contrast** (title framing, Intro (ii), contribution (2)). Not isolated (F01, F13). With an NN regressor, the POD output is worse.
2. **"Controlled mechanism tests that separate subspace, kernel prior, anisotropy and hyper-parameter fitting"** (Section 1 contributions, Section 3). Subspace is never separated; D conflates fitting method with optimiser resolution (F05); E is pending and alone does not isolate fitting from anisotropy.
3. **"Common/matched tuning protocol"** (abstract-level phrases). The budget is a count of draws, per method (LS 24 vs NL 16 trials), with different per-trial optimisation and wall-clock, and the draws are shared across all cells (F03, F04).
4. **"H4a confirmed" / "unit of inference is the instance"** (Section 4 Statistics): cell-level pre-registered p is anti-conservative; at the instance level it is not significant after Holm (F09). The two statements are inconsistent.
5. **"Decisive" LS wins of 2 to 3 decades.** They coincide with an NN accuracy floor ~1e-3 (F02) and both-below-1% cells (F20). No evidence yet that they reflect data efficiency or representation.
6. **"NL catches up with data" and any N-trend** is confounded with batch/step-count at N=1024 (F02, F21).
7. **Any reading of Exp A as "diagnostic fails/does not work"**, or the pooled 24-instance LOFO as support for it. The test is underpowered (power ≈ 0.5 at the pre-specified effect), driven by nlheat (ρ turns +0.49 without it), clustered (4 families), and the pooled result is partly circular (features selected on v1) (F06, F15).
8. **A2's Q1 as written** (when it is analysed): the p0 is miscounted (3/20), the threshold is hindsight-placed 0.003 from burgers_L1, the 7 qualifiers are 4 families, and f1 is confounded with p = 3 (F07, F08).
9. **"Convergence" via loss curves.** Cosine-to-zero guarantees a minimum-at-end curve (F02).
10. **"The search space is not binding"** (`search_space_boundary.json`): not supportable with shared draws (F18).
11. **"Preregistered in each stage"** without a prospective/retrospective qualification for Stages 2 and 3 (F14), and the "32 instances" count, which is really 8 + 4 + 4 = 16 families.

## 4. Alternative explanations not yet tested (ranked)

1. **GP regressor vs POD subspace** (F01): no-POD GP/KRR; POD output with fixed regressor already favours direct output.
2. **NN optimisation floor** (precision, optimiser, LR schedule) rather than representation or data (F02): train-vs-test errors, L-BFGS polish, float64, step-matched protocol.
3. **Step-count/batch rule at N=1024** (F02).
4. **Common-mode search luck** (F03).
5. **Parameter dimension and smoothness as the true driver of f1 and of GP success** (F07). All low-f1 instances have p = 3.
6. **Budget-curve extrapolation**: B > 8 for NN; compute-matched tuning (F04).
7. **Family size and membership asymmetry** (3 vs 2 methods; pod_mlp inside LS) (F13).
8. **Absolute-accuracy equivalence** rather than relative log-ratio (F20).
9. **Centred / variance-normalised error metric** (F22).
10. **NL family too narrow** (no nonlinear-manifold ROMs, no stronger NN recipes) (F19).
11. **GP implementation specifics**: marginal likelihood fitted only for the first 4 modes on ≤ 300 points, other modes reuse the 4th kernel, single start, ~1.4% failures (`sensitivity_gp_repair.json` shows small effects); not a threat to the direction of conclusions.

## Five-line summary of the most important findings

1. **F01 (CRITICAL):** The "LS" advantage is a POD+GP advantage. With the regressor fixed, direct MLP beats POD+MLP in 17/24 instances at N=1024, so the "subspace vs nonlinear output" question and the "separated mechanisms" claim are unsupported by the design.
2. **F02 + F03 (CRITICAL):** The NN side looks optimisation-limited (error never below 6.6e-4 in 144 cells; flat learning curves; a 2× step-count jump at N=1024 that produces an N=512→1024 kink in 23/24 instances), and the "random search" uses the identical 7 configs across all instances and N within a seed. The 3-decade "decisive" wins and the N-trend are therefore not interpretable as data-efficiency effects, and the effective tuning replication is about 3 draws.
3. **F06 (MAJOR):** Exp A is underpowered (power ≈ 0.52 at the pre-specified ρ = -0.64) and its signal is entirely nlheat (ρ = +0.49 without it; family-level exact minimum p = 0.042; P2 exact Holm = 0.0504, knife-edge). "Not replicated" is formally right but should read "inconclusive".
4. **F08 + F07 (MAJOR):** A2's Q1 is mis-calibrated: p0 should be 3/20, not 2/20 (k ≥ 3 then gives p = 0.074), the 7 qualifiers are 4 families (all-or-none model gives P(k ≥ 3) = 0.052), and f1 < -2 is confounded with p = 3 in 6 of 7 qualifiers and the control.
5. **F09/F10/F14 (MAJOR/MODERATE):** H4a is "confirmed" only at the cell level (instance-level Holm 0.125) and counts ties as wins; the 0.10 tie band equals seed noise (sign consistency 71% v1, 50% Exp A); the preregistration is genuinely prospective only for Stage 1 (Stage 3 was committed 5 minutes after Exp A results, on a self-hosted history). Validation-based selection and small-N validation size are NOT problems here (regret ≤ 0.02 decades).
